##Hospital Patient Analytics Dashboard

A professional Excel analytics dashboard designed to monitor hospital operational efficiency, financial performance, patient demographics, and department metrics. Built using a dataset of 1,000 patient records, this project transforms raw administrative data into actionable operational insights.

Project Overview

This dashboard provides executive-level visibility into key performance indicators (KPIs) and operational metrics across key hospital operational areas:

Financial Tracking: Monitoring total billings, actual payments collected, outstanding balances, and payment status breakdowns.

Department Performance: Comparative analysis of patient volume and revenue generation by department.

Operational Trends: Analysis of monthly admission patterns and patient throughput.

Patient Demographics & Clinical Metrics: Insights into patient age distribution, length of stay (LOS), and insurance coverage patterns.

Key Features & Visualizations

1. Key Performance Indicators (KPI Cards)

Total Hospital Billing: Aggregated revenue across all patient visits.

Total Outstanding Revenue: Balance remaining across pending and partial payment accounts.

Total Patient Visits: Total patient admissions recorded (1,000 records).

Average Length of Stay (LOS): Overall average stay duration across departments.

Average Insurance Coverage: Mean insurance contribution per patient visit.

2. Analytical Visualizations

📈 Monthly Patient Admissions: Trend chart visualizing admission volume fluctuations over time.

🍕 Payment Status Breakdown: Proportional breakdown of accounts (Paid, Partially Paid, Pending).

📊 Department Performance: Dual-metric analysis comparing patient volume and total billed revenue across clinical departments.

📊 Patient Visits by Age Group: Demographic analysis mapping visit frequency across age distributions.
